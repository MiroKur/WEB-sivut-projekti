Design Insights & Trends

Design 5 min read

Finding clarity in a world of visual noise

Read insight ↗

Good design creates a clear path. Start with one primary message, give it room to breathe, and use contrast to guide the eye. Removing a distraction can be more valuable than adding another feature.

Inspiration 5 min read

What architecture can teach us about interfaces

Read insight ↗

Rhythm, proportion and hierarchy help people find their way in both buildings and interfaces. A consistent grid makes a design feel familiar, while a carefully chosen break in the pattern gives it character.

Process 5 min read

Designing experiences that feel a little more human

Read insight ↗

A human experience starts with listening. Use familiar language, create generous touch targets and make the next step clear. Test the result on a small screen and with a keyboard, then refine the details.
